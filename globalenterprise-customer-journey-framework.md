## 1. Executive Summary

Global Enterprise is a B2B advisory and technology firm serving senior leaders at institutions facing complex transformation challenges. This framework maps the complete customer journey from initial problem recognition through engagement, delivery, and long-term partnership.

### Key Insights:
- **Multiple Personas:** Different leaders (CEO, CIO, CTO, COO, Public-sector, Data/AI leaders) enter with distinct problems
- **Path Differentiation:** Each persona requires different content, evidence, and trust signals
- **Complex Decision Process:** B2B advisory purchases involve multiple stakeholders (Procurement, Security, Legal, Finance)
- **High-Stakes Engagement:** Customers need proof of expertise before initiating contact

## 3. End-to-End Customer Journey Stages

### **Stage 1: Trigger / Problem**
*Before the website visit*
- **Customer Situation:** Organizational challenge emerges (transformation need, AI adoption, modernization)
- **Customer Need:** Clarity about the problem and what to do next
- **Emotional State:** Uncertainty, pressure, awareness of organizational gap

### **Stage 2: Awareness**
*Initial discovery of Global Enterprise*
- **Touchpoints:** Google search, LinkedIn, industry referrals, reports, events
- **Customer Question:** "Who is Global Enterprise and can they help someone like me?"
- **Website Entry Points:** Homepage, industry pages, insights, search results

### **Stage 3: Discovery**
*Exploring the website*
- **Routes:**
  - **Capability Route:** "What can you do?" → Services pages
  - **Industry Route:** "Do you understand my environment?" → Industries pages
  - **Problem Route:** "I have this particular challenge." → Solutions pages
  - **Knowledge Route:** "I want to learn before I talk." → Insights, reports
- **Customer Goal:** Understand scope of offerings

### **Stage 4: Problem Understanding**
*Clarifying the actual need*
- **Customer Realization:** "We need AI" → but need could be AI strategy, operating model, implementation, or cost management
- **Website Role:** Help customer identify which capability is relevant to their specific problem
- **Critical Juncture:** Moving from general problem to specific solution need

### **Stage 5: Find Relevant Solution**
*Matching need to Global Enterprise capabilities*

| Customer Problem | Customer Need | Relevant GE Capability | Expected Outcome |
|------------------|---------------|------------------------|------------------|
| AI strategy is unclear | Strategic direction | Enterprise AI & ML Labs | Clear AI roadmap |
| AI projects aren't scaling | Operating capability | Operating Model Design | Scalable operating model |
| Data/AI costs are increasing | Cost visibility/control | Data Labs & AI Cost Management | Better economics |
| Legacy technology is holding organization back | Modernization | Technology/Data capabilities | Modern technology foundation |
| Employees aren't adopting transformation | Change capability | Leadership & Talent / ITIL Change | Adoption and organizational readiness |
| Leadership lacks future visibility | Foresight | Quantum Intelligence & Foresight | Better strategic decisions |
| Organization needs digital transformation | Transformation strategy | Operating Model / Technology | Transformation roadmap |
| Product teams need new capabilities | Product/technology capability | AI/ML + Data + Strategy | Stronger product capability |

### **Stage 6: Evaluation**
*"Why Global Enterprise instead of another consultancy?"*
- **Evidence Needs:**
  - Expertise: "Do they understand my problem?"
  - Experience: "Have they done this before?"
  - Outcomes: "Can they actually produce results?"
  - People: "Who will work with us?"
  - Credibility: "Can I trust them?"

### **Stage 7: Look for Proof**
*Different proof for different personas*

| Persona | Proof Requirements |
|---------|-------------------|
| **CIO** | Technology expertise, AI/data experience, transformation examples, senior technical expertise |
| **CFO/Finance** | Economics, cost control, business value, ROI |
| **COO** | Operating model, execution, process improvement, change management |
| **CTO** | Technical depth, AI/ML, data architecture, emerging technology |
| **CEO** | Strategic thinking, business outcomes, future positioning, leadership credibility |
| **Public-sector** | Trust, governance, security, institutional understanding, resilience |

### **Stage 8: Build Trust**
*"Can I trust these people with an important organizational problem?"*
- **Trust Signals:**
  - Team page (senior expertise)
  - About page (company positioning/history)
  - Selected Work (evidence of previous work)
  - Insights/Reports (intellectual expertise)
  - Trust Center/FAQ (addressing concerns)

### **Stage 9: Risk / Procurement Validation**
*Internal stakeholder review*
- **Concerns:**
  - Procurement: "Are they legitimate and compliant?"
  - Security: "How will our information be handled?"
  - Legal: "Contract terms, IP, liability?"
  - Finance: "Is this worth the investment?"

### **Stage 10: Contact / Principal Dialogue**
*Primary conversion point*
- **Customer State:** "I think Global Enterprise might be able to help us. Let's talk."
- **Contact Form:** Request a Principal Dialogue
- **Emotional State:** Cautious optimism, ready for next step

### **Stage 11: Initial Conversation**
*Qualification and discovery*
- **Global Enterprise Process:** Inquiry → Acknowledgement → Internal qualification → Principal conversation
- **Customer Goal:** Assess fit, understand approach, gauge chemistry
- **Outcome:** Problem discovery and potential solution alignment

### **Stage 12: Proposal / Engagement**
*Formal partnership formation*
- **Deliverable:** Customized proposal addressing specific institutional challenge
- **Stakeholders:** Multiple internal approvals often required
- **Timeline:** Can be weeks to months depending on organization size

### **Stage 13: Delivery**
*Implementation and execution*
- **Engagement Types:**
  - Principal mandate session (strategy)
  - Intelligence system diagnostic (2-4 weeks)
  - Institutional transformation partnership (long-horizon)
- **Success Metrics:** Measurable outcomes, capability transfer

### **Stage 14: Long-term Relationship**
*Beyond the engagement*
- **Retention:** "Did Global Enterprise deliver what we needed?"
- **Expansion:** "What else can they help us with?"
- **Advocacy:** "Would we recommend them to another organization?"

## 5. Website Touchpoint Analysis

### **Critical Conversion Paths**

1. **Problem → Capability Mapping**
   - **Current:** Services pages show capabilities but don't guide problem diagnosis
   - **Opportunity:** Interactive diagnostic tool: "What problem are you trying to solve?" → Recommended capability

2. **Persona-Specific Entry Points**
   - **Current:** Generic navigation, all personas see same homepage
   - **Opportunity:** Persona-based landing pages or content clusters

3. **Evidence Presentation**
   - **Current:** Case studies (only 3), evidence band, insights
   - **Opportunity:** More detailed case studies by industry/persona, client testimonials, measurable outcomes

4. **Trust Building**
   - **Current:** Team page, about, FAQ
   - **Opportunity:** Trust center with security protocols, compliance information, engagement process details

### **Conversion Friction Points**

| Friction Point | Impact | Recommendation |
|----------------|--------|----------------|
| **Limited case studies** (only 3) | Reduces proof for diverse industries | Add more case studies covering different industries/capabilities |
| **No client testimonials** | Missing social proof | Add brief client quotes with permission |
| **Generic contact form** | Doesn't capture context | Form fields for: persona type, problem category, organization size |
| **Missing ROI evidence** | Finance stakeholders need cost justification | Add business value/ROI case studies |
| **No security/compliance details** | Blocks security/legal stakeholders | Add trust center with security protocols, compliance info |

- **Doubt → Trust:** Transparent processes, case studies with measurable outcomes
- **Apprehension → Action:** Low-friction next steps, clear expectations

## 20. Crisis/Reputation Management Journey

### **Crisis Impact on Customer Journey**
1. **Immediate (0-24 hours):** Trust erosion, increased scrutiny
2. **Short-term (1-7 days):** Evaluation pause, alternative research
3. **Medium-term (1-4 weeks):** Cautious re-engagement, heightened proof requirements
4. **Long-term (1-6 months):** Gradual trust rebuilding, extra verification

### **Trust-Rebuilding Mechanisms**
1. **Transparency:** Open communication about issues
