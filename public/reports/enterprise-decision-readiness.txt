Global Enterprise Operating Report
Prepared as a practical, reusable working reference.

Executive summary
This decision-readiness report compares mandate clarity, data trust, AI governance, and capability ownership.

Key checkpoints
1) Is the decision authority explicit for each workstream?
2) Are data sources discoverable, versioned, and accessible where the decision is made?
3) Is the AI workflow linked to a human exception path and documented recovery actions?
4) Are modernization dependencies visible before execution commitments?

Outcome
Use this report as a first-pass diagnostic for modernization portfolios.
