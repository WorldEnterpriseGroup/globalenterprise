Global Enterprise Modernization and Investment Priority Brief
Sequencing guidance for legacy modernization and operating-model evolution.

Focus

How to use

- Make explicit the continuity requirement before technical design decisions.
- Assign ownership for each transition and close the loop through measurable outcomes.
