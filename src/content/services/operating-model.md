The operating model is where strategy becomes real. We help leaders make decision rights, accountability, and ways of working legible across the enterprise.

Our work can include enterprise design, policy and procedure, governance, service blueprints, and the change architecture required to make the new model stick.

## What we actually change

An operating model engagement should leave more than a target-state diagram. It should make the organization better at choosing, coordinating, and learning.

We typically work through:

- a **mandate map** that connects strategic intent to outcomes;
- a **decision-rights architecture** that shows who can decide, advise, execute, and stop;
- a **service and process blueprint** that exposes the handoffs creating delay;
- a **governance rhythm** that turns exceptions into learning instead of escalation theater;
- an **adoption plan** that gives teams a usable next default.

The [UN Sustainable Development Goals Report 2026](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/report/2026/) makes the leadership requirement current: progress is meaningful but insufficient, and technology, data access, energy transition, and international cooperation need to move faster before 2030. The same discipline applies in commercial, healthcare, and public enterprises: the system changes when decisions, funding, service ownership, and behavior change together.
